# Product Overview  
StockMate is a browser-based inventory management application designed to help small shops and buisnesses manage their product stock.  
The application allows users to add new products, update stock, monitor inventory, and receive notifications when their products are running low.   
StockMate helps simplify inventory management by providing their users with a place to organize and monitor all of the stock going in and out.  

## Who is StockMate for?  
StockMate is designed with small shops and buisnesses in mind. For the people who need a simple and easier way to manage their product inventory.  
It is intended for buisness owners and employees keep track of stock levels, updating quanities of products, and monitoring inventory.  
StockMate is useful for shops that want to keepp their inventory organised without needing complicated inventory management software.  

## Key Features 

### Product Management  
Users are able to add new products, edit product details, and remove products.  

### Tracking Stock  
Users can update product quantity when new stock comes in or is sold.  

### Notifications  
Users will be alerted when a product reaches the minimum stock level. This helps buisnesses know when they need to reorder new products.  

### Search and Filtering  
Users are able to search for specific products and filter their inventory to find items easily.  

### Inventory Overview  
Users can view their current inventory from a central dashboard.  

## How it works  
StockMate is accessed through a web browser. Users are able to sign in to their buisness account and access their inventory dashboard.

On the dashboard, users can view their inventory, search for specific products, and monitor stock levels. To add a product, users enter details such as its name, description, and available quantity. They can update product information and quantities as stock arrives or items are sold. 
 
For every product, the user will be able to set a minimum stock level. When the quantity reaches that level, StockMate will send an alert to users that they need to reorder. 

## Requirements  
Users will need the following:  

### Technical Requirements  
- **Internet connection:** A stable internet connection is required to use StockMate.  
- **Web browser:** StockMate can bbe accessed through a web browser such as Google.  
- **Device:** A computer, laptop, or a tablet.  

### Account and Inventory  
- **StockMate account:** Users need a registered account to access the appplication.  
- **Buisness information:** Users should add their buisness details when setting up their account.  
- **Product information:** Users should have details of the products they want to add, such as product names and stock quantity. 

# Getting Started   
To start using StockMate, users need to create an account for their buisness.  
Once registered, users can sign in through their web browser to access their inventory dashboard.  

Before managing inventory, users should:  
1. Set up their buisness account.
2. Create individual empployee accounts if required.
3. Add their products and stock quantities.
4. Set the minimum stock levels to recieve notifications.

For detailed instructions on adding and uppdating products, refer to the **Adding and Updating Stock** guide.
