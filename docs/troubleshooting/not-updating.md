# Troubleshooting: Products Won't Update
Use this guide when a product's details or quantity do notupdate after a change.

## Quick Checks
Before tying to update again:  
1. Check that you are signed in to the correct StockMate buisness account.
2. Confirm that your device has a working internet connection.
3. Refresh the inventory dashboard nd check if the change has apppeared.
4. Search for the product again and confirm you've opened the correct product record. 

## Save Does Not Complete
If StockMate appears to keep saving or does not confirm the change:  
1. Wait a short time in case the update is still processing. 
2. Check your internet connection.
3. Avoid submitting the same change repeatedly as the first attempt may still be processing.
4. Refresh the page and check the product's current details before tying again.
5. If the change is still not appearing, reopen the product, make the change again, and save it.

## Product Details Are Not Changing  
If the product name, descripption, or other details remain the same:  
1. Open the product record and choose the edit option.  
2. Make sure the correct field has been changed.  
3. Check that any required fields contain information.  
4. Save the changes.
5. Return to your inventory dashboard, search for the product,, and confirm the details.  

## Stock Quantity is Incorrect
If a stock update does not appear or shows the wrong quantity:  
1. Check whether StockMate asks for the new total quantity or the amount being added or removed.  
2. Confirm the quantity you entered is correct.  
3. Save the update and check the product record again.  
4. If the quantity is still incorrect, update it again using the current stock on hand as your reference.  

Do not repeat a change until you know whether the earlier change was applied. Otherwise, the quantity could be changed more than once.   

## Update Appears on One Screen but Not Another  
1. Refresh the inventory dashboard.
2. Search for the product again to view its record.
3. If the dashboard still shows different information, sign out and back in, then check again.
4. If the difference remains, note which screen shows each value and contact your StockMate administrator or contact customer support.  

## Still Can't Update the Product   
If these steps do not resolve the issue, contact your StockMate administrator or contact customer support. Include: 

- The product name.
- What you were trying to change.
- The time the issue occurred.
- Any error message shown.
- Any steps you've already tried.  

Please do not include your password in a suppport request. If the product is an urgent decision, verify the quantity using your buisness's current records until the StockMate record is corrected.
